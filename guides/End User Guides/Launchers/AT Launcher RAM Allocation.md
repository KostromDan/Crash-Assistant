# How to Manage Memory Settings in AT Launcher

To configure RAM allocation in AT Launcher:

1. Open the **ATLauncher**.
2. Click on the **Settings** button in the right-hand sidebar.
3. Navigate to the **Java/Minecraft** tab at the top.
4. Find the **Maximum Memory/RAM** setting.
5. Enter the desired amount of RAM in megabytes (e.g., 4096 for 4GB, 8192 for 8GB).
6. Click the **Save** button at the bottom.
