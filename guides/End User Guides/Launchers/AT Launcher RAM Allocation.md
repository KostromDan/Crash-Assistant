# How to Manage Memory Settings in AT Launcher

To change the amount of RAM allocated to a specific instance in AT Launcher:

1. Open AT Launcher, go to the **Instances** tab, and click the **Settings** button on your instance.
<img width="1259" height="1023" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8db5f666-17dd-4345-986b-2d201269929f" />

2. In the settings window, navigate to the **Java/Minecraft** tab.
3. Locate the **Maximum Memory/Ram** field and adjust the value to your needs (e.g., 4096).
<img width="1259" height="1023" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e3661963-22b7-47ee-8a85-fbea7094ad12" />

4. Click **Save** at the bottom to apply the changes.
