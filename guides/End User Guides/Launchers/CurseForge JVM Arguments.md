# How to Manage JVM Arguments for a Specific Profile in CurseForge

To add or change JVM arguments for a specific Minecraft profile:

1. Open the **CurseForge** app and navigate to the profile you want to modify.
2. Click on the **three vertical dots** (menu icon) next to the **Play** button and select **Profile Options**.
<img width="1259" height="1023" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b2f494e7-090c-4a04-a8b6-3a40e03349b6" />

3. In the "Profile Options" window, **scroll down** to the **Advanced Settings** section at the bottom.
4. Locate the **Additional Arguments** text box. Here you can add or modify your JVM flags.
<img width="1259" height="1023" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cf93c329-0c56-4c3d-bbd3-36a5bdda12c2" />

5. Click **Done** to save your changes.
