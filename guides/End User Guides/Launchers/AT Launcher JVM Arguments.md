# How to Manage JVM Arguments in AT Launcher

To configure JVM arguments in AT Launcher:

1. Open **ATLauncher**.
2. Click the **Settings** button in the sidebar.
3. Select the **Java/Minecraft** tab.
4. Locate the **JVM Arguments** text field.
5. Add your custom flags separated by spaces.
6. Click **Save** at the bottom.
